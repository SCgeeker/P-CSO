# Pinker Syntax — 範例集（每條規則僅留最難的 1 例）

## Rule 1: Reduce Memory Load
❌ "The hypothesis that classifiers modulate mental simulation during sentence comprehension was tested."
✅ "We tested the hypothesis that classifiers modulate mental simulation during sentence comprehension."

## Rule 2: Avoid Parsing Collapse
❌ "The participants who completed the task that the experimenter who trained them designed performed well."
✅ "The experimenter trained participants and designed a task. Participants who completed this task performed well."

## Rule 3: Optimize Information Density
❌ "There are many factors that influence comprehension."
✅ "Many factors influence comprehension."

## Rule 4: Satisfy Expectation Ordering
❌ "A profiling mechanism that activates detailed shape features during comprehension explains these results."
✅ "These results are explained by a profiling mechanism that activates detailed shape features during comprehension."
（輕主題「results」先出現；重、新的機制延後）

## Rule 5: Avoid Ambiguity
❌ "We tested participants with the new paradigm in the lab."
✅ "In the lab, we tested participants with the new paradigm."

## Rule 6: Check Grammatical Consistency
❌ "The analysis of multiple experiments **suggest** a robust effect."
✅ "The analysis of multiple experiments **suggests** a robust effect."

## Rule 7: 不用 em-dash 連接子句
❌ "The effect is bounded within 1–4 objects — the range of subitization."
✅ "The effect is bounded within 1–4 objects. This range corresponds to subitization."

## Rule 8: 不用括號放行內列表
❌ "Three features (shape, orientation, color) were tested."
✅ "Three features were tested, including shape, orientation, and color."

## Rule 9: 不用關係子句
❌ "The effect, which was replicated in Experiment 2, supports the hypothesis."
✅ "The effect supports the hypothesis. It was replicated in Experiment 2."

## Rule 10: 能用直接句就不用從屬子句
❌ "Although the distance effect was absent, the congruency effect remained robust."
✅ "The distance effect was absent. The congruency effect remained robust."

## Pinker Chapter 4 Few-Shot（原 5 例僅留最難 1 例：平行結構）

BEFORE: "The authors propose that distinct selection pressures have influenced cognitive abilities and personality traits, and that intelligence differences are the result of mutation-selection balance, while balancing selection accounts for personality differences."

AFTER: "The authors propose that distinct selection pressures have influenced cognitive abilities and personality traits: mutation-selection balance accounts for differences in cognitive ability, whereas balancing selection accounts for differences in personality traits."

RULE：把兩個子句鎖進相同的因果順序，並統一用詞，讓平行結構真正對稱（這是 Pinker 原書中最複雜的一組修訂，涉及重新排列 + 統一術語 + 對稱句法三重操作）。

## Rule 11：連接詞負荷

**Before**（一句七個 and，其中兩處實為對比）

> The account divides at exactly this point, **and** the division matters for what follows.
> Diekelmann and Born separate slow-wave sleep from REM **and** reach the opposite
> conclusion about consolidation. … Declarative memories improve after early-night sleep
> **and** not after late-night sleep, **and** procedural memories improve after late-night
> sleep **and** not after early-night sleep. … Walker cites Diekelmann and Born for the claim
> that all sleep consolidates declarative memory, **and** the source says the benefit is
> stage-specific.

**After**（and 減為三個；兩處對比改用 but，判準另起一句）

> The account divides at exactly this point. Diekelmann and Born separate slow-wave sleep from
> REM, **and** they reach the opposite conclusion about consolidation. … Two behavioural
> dissociations carry the separation. Declarative memories improve after early-night sleep
> **but** not after late-night sleep … Procedural memories improve after late-night sleep
> **but** not after early-night sleep. … Walker cites Diekelmann and Born for the claim that
> all sleep consolidates declarative memory, **but** the source says the benefit is
> stage-specific.

**要點**：最後那個 and 才是關鍵。整段存在的理由就是「引用與來源相牴觸」，
用加合連接詞會把這個矛盾說成兩件並列的事實。四個 and 串起兩組對比則是第一種子型：
文法無誤，但讀者看不出哪裡才是轉折。
